## Brand & Style

This design system powers a high-energy, premium creator education ecosystem tailored for emerging digital entrepreneurs and content creators. It bridges the gap between high-converting creator economy energy and high-trust SaaS precision.

### Personality & Tone
- **Electric Authority:** Energetic, motivational, and unapologetically growth-oriented, grounded by systematic curriculum structures and empirical milestones.
- **Modern Creator Native:** Fluent in current social media aesthetics, employing sunset gradients, crisp dark-slate anchors, and luminous micro-accents.
- **Empowering & Action-Oriented:** Strips away academic fluff in favor of immediate progress feedback, bite-sized roadmaps, and measurable revenue-generating tasks.

### Design Movement
The visual language merges **Sleek SaaS Precision** with **Vibrant Layered Glassmorphism**. Dark neutral canvases (#0F172A) anchor bright editorial cards, micro-glows, and vibrant gradient tracks (deep violet through fuchsia to amber). The feel is high-end digital native: crisp hairline dividers, deep ambient colored backdrops, and responsive interactive modules.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around Instagram's iconic sunset spectrum, refined through a deep dark-mode framework to eliminate eye fatigue while magnifying conversion drivers and progression metrics.

### Key Tokens & Functional Roles
- **Primary (`#E1306C` - Radiant Fuchsia):** Applied to primary actions, interactive toggle states, dynamic progress rings, and active roadmap stops.
- **Secondary (`#FCAF45` - Sunset Amber):** High-impact callouts, streak badges, monetization metrics, and forward momentum milestones.
- **Tertiary (`#833AB4` - Royal Violet):** Deep structural gradients, module container headers, and elevated background glows.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#0F172A` - Slate Deep):** Core workspace canvas, framing interfaces with deep slate contrast.
- **Neutral Surface Elevated (`#1E293B` - Slate Card):** First-tier elevated cards, lesson modules, and panel containers.
- **Neutral Surface Higher (`#334155` - Slate Muted):** Border strokes, chip containers, and inactive progress bars.
- **Text Dominance:** `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) for maximum legibility headers and primary data; `#94A3B8` (Slate 400) for instructional body copy and structural metadata.

### Gradients
- **Brand Signature Spectrum:** Linear `135deg, #833AB4 0%, #FD1D1D 50%, #FCAF45 100%` used on primary CTA fills, active roadmap path strokes, and hero title highlights.
- **Luminous Surface Tint:** Linear `180deg, rgba(225, 48, 108, 0.08) 0%, rgba(15, 23, 42, 0) 100%` over elevated dark cards to convey ambient lighting.

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** is the single typographic voice across headlines, body copy, and UI badges. Its geometric balance, modern aperture openings, and high x-height combine high-yield legibility on screens with the approachable, energetic character critical to creator platform engagement.

### Hierarchy & Editorial Rules
- **Display & Hero Treatments:** Weight 800 with tight negative tracking (`-0.03em`) creates athletic, forward-leaning punchiness. When overlaying gradient text fills, apply weight 800 strictly.
- **Section Headers:** Scaled responsively via mobile tokens. Ensure headline pairings with secondary badges preserve a clean 8px vertical offset.
- **Micro-Labels & Metric Pills:** Weight 700 with positive letter spacing (`0.06em`) in all-caps formatting for metadata tags, difficulty levels, and estimated completion times.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system uses a 12-column responsive fluid grid pinned to a maximum content width of 1280px to guarantee focused dashboard navigation and high scanning efficiency across module roadmaps.

### Form Factors & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout. Gutters compress to 1rem (`16px`), with 1rem (`16px`) outer margins. Navigation pivots to a bottom-anchored mobile dock for thumb-reach ergonomics.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout. Gutters expand to 1.25rem (`20px`), margins scale to 1.5rem (`24px`). Lesson roadmaps switch from dual cards to single-column interactive timelines.
- **Desktop (>= 1025px):** 12-column layout. Full 1.5rem (`24px`) gutters and 2rem (`32px`) margins. Sidebar tracking, module checklists, and dynamic revenue simulator widgets occupy dedicated side columns (4-col / 8-col splits).

### Rhythmic Discipline
Interior card paddings standardize strictly to `space-md` (`16px`) for mobile cards and `space-lg` (`24px`) for desktop milestone modules. Element stack gaps within cards never exceed `space-sm` (`8px`) between headers and body descriptions to maintain tight optical grouping.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on harsh dropshadows. Depth is articulated through **Tonal Layering**, **Atmospheric Backdrops**, and **Subtle Rim Lighting**.

### Elevation Stack
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Flat `#0F172A`. Zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Module Cards & Layout Panels):** Surface `#1E293B` overlaid with a 1px continuous border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Active State):** Surface `#24334A` surrounded by a dual outline: an outer ambient blur of `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(225, 48, 108, 0.18)` and an inner 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays & Sticky Docks):** Backdrop-filter `blur(16px)` over `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.8)`. Outer boundary: 1px border gradient `linear-gradient(to bottom, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2), rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02))`. Deep directional shadow `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.

### Luminous Ambient Glows
Roadmap anchor nodes and completion badges feature an unfocused radial underglow (`filter: blur(28px)`, opacity 30%) tinted to `#E1306C` or `#FCAF45`, elevating key victories and next-step actions above static text.

## Shapes

The design system implements **Level 2 (Rounded)** shape geometry. This produces modern, polished component silhouettes that align with consumer app polish without sacrificing data density.

### Radius Assignments
- **Base Components (`rounded`, 0.5rem / 8px):** Form inputs, tooltips, list item selections, and nested checklist checkboxes.
- **Cards & Step Containers (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px):** Module overview blocks, milestone containers, and media player wrappers.
- **Pill Badges & Master Action Buttons (`rounded-full`, 9999px):** Status badges ("MODUL 1", "WIP", "SELESAI"), metric indicators, and primary conversion CTAs utilize pure pill geometry to stand out from rectangular content panels.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Cuan Trigger):** Pill-shaped, gradient fill (`135deg, #833AB4, #E1306C, #FD1D1D`), text `#FFFFFF` in `label-lg`. Micro-interaction: `transform: scale(0.98)` on active press; hover triggers a soft outward fuchsia glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(225, 48, 108, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary (Module Resume):** Surface `#1E293B`, crisp border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `#F8FAFC`. On hover, border transitions to `#E1306C` and background lightens to `#24334A`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** No border, transparent background, text `#94A3B8`. Hover states shift text color to `#FCAF45`.

### Chips & Badge Pills
- **Status Tags:** Pill shape, padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`. 
  - *Completed:* `background: rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.1)`, `border: 1px solid rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.25)`, text `#4ADE80`.
  - *Current Module (Hot):* `background: rgba(225, 48, 108, 0.12)`, `border: 1px solid rgba(225, 48, 108, 0.35)`, text `#F43F5E`.
  - *Monetization Metric Pill:* `background: rgba(252, 175, 69, 0.1)`, `border: 1px solid rgba(252, 175, 69, 0.3)`, text `#FBBF24`.

### Progress Meters & Step Trackers
- **Progress Track:** Height `8px`, track background `#334155`, fill is an animated gradient (`#833AB4` to `#FCAF45`). Rounded ends.
- **Milestone Node (Roadmap):** 40px circle. Unlocked state has a 2px rim of `#E1306C` with central step digit; locked state has `#334155` background with padlock icon; completed state is full gradient fill with white checkmark.

### Interactive Module Cards
- **Structure:** Level 1 elevation, `#1E293B` background with 16px inner padding. Includes header row with step badge ("FASE 01: PONDASI AKUN") and estimated time ("25 Menit"). Body contains `headline-sm` title and 2 lines of summary text. Bottom bar contains actionable sub-task progress indicator (e.g., "3/5 Tugas Selesai").
- **State Changes:** Hovering initiates a subtle vertical lift (`-2px`) and shifts the top 1px border highlight from subtle white to brand fuchsia.

### Form Inputs
- **Fields:** Height `48px`, background `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.6)`, border `1px solid #334155`, text `#F8FAFC`. Focus ring is an energetic double-edge: `border-color: #E1306C` and `outline: 2px solid rgba(225, 48, 108, 0.2)`. Placeholder text in `#64748B`.

### Checkboxes & Action Lists
- **Checkboxes:** 20px rounded squares (`rounded`, 4px). Unchecked: `#1E293B` with border `#475569`. Checked: solid gradient `#E1306C` to `#FD1D1D` with white checkmark.
- **Roadmap Checklist Item:** Flex row with checkbox, label, and downloadable resource tag ("Template Bio.pdf"). On checked, label adopts `text-decoration: line-through` and text drops to `#64748B`.
